# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-11

First release.

### Timer
- Timer systems: 25/5, 45/15, 50/10, 90/20 and custom presets with automatic saving
- Auto switch between focus and break, with a long break after a set number of sessions
- Start, pause, resume, reset and skip, with in-app shortcuts: Space and R
- Smart notifications: sound, in-app visual alerts and system notifications

### Tasks and projects
- Daily, weekly and monthly tasks, plus a projects system
- Every task is marked as work or study
- Difficulty (easy, medium, hard) with an estimated number of pomodoros, and priorities
- Drag and drop ordering and deadlines with overdue alerts
- A task is required before the timer can start

### Progress
- Daily and weekly streaks, longest streak and streak freeze (2 per month)
- XP and levels that increase with every completed pomodoro
- 33 badges
- Task analytics: estimated time vs actual time and completed pomodoros

### Summaries
- Daily summary with total focus time, pomodoros, completed tasks, distraction rate and an hourly chart
- Weekly summary with day comparison, best and weakest day, trend and weekly goal
- Monthly summary with an activity heatmap, total hours, completed projects and streak evolution
- Custom date range of up to 3 months
- Every summary can be split into work and study

### Sounds
- Synthesized ambient sounds: rain, ocean, wind, fireplace, birds, white, pink and brown noise and a calm pad
- Sound effects that can be switched off independently from ambient sounds

### Look and feel
- 13 built-in themes, a custom theme builder and a random theme generator
- Custom fonts, timer size, sidebar position, timer layout and background image
- Compact window that can stay on top of other programs
- Arabic and English interface with right-to-left support

### Smart helpers
- Best focus time based on your history
- Break suggestions such as stretching, breathing and movement
- Water reminders
- Motivational quotes and your own goal reminders, each with its own display time
- Export and import backups
